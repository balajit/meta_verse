### **Directory Mapping to Open-Source Stack**

* **`namespace/`:** Utilizes **NetworkX** inside `tree_resolver.py` for DAG inheritance graph logic and **OPA** in `config/opa_policies/` for rule evaluation.


* **`merger/`:** Integrates **`jsonmerge`**, **`jsonpatch`**, **Pydantic V2**, and **Apache Hamilton** across `deep_merger.py`, `dynamic_schema_builder.py`, and `contract_checker.py`.


* **`fsm/`:** Leverages **`python-statemachine`** in `lifecycle_guard.py` and **SQLAlchemy Async** optimistic locking in `state_persister.py`.


* **`hydrator/`:** Houses **Ruamel.yaml**, **JSONSchema**, and **Hashlib** utilities to parse, validate, and hash manifests prior to DB persistence.
